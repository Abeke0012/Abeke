## Prompt

One continuous seamless cinematic shot, no cuts, no fades, no black frames. Premium food commercial for the burger brand "SELF".

0–3s: Start exactly on the start image: a glossy brioche-bun burger with caramelized onions, melted cheddar, a seared beef patty and creamy sauce, centered on a vivid orange gradient studio background. The burger slowly rotates, then spins faster and lifts into the air. The camera pushes in and orbits around it.

3–6s: In mid-air the burger explodes vertically into floating layers, matching reference image 1 (bottom to top): bottom bun, creamy sauce, grilled beef patty, melted cheddar slice, caramelized onions, top brioche bun. The cheddar stretches before it separates. Steam rises from the hot patty and fine crumbs float in the air. The motion is controlled and physical.

6–9s: The layers fall back down one after another and snap into the finished burger. Gravity pulls the burger down and the camera tracks it in one fluid move. The orange studio turns into the warm rustic wooden table from reference image 2. The burger lands softly in the center of the open kraft cardboard box.

9–12s: The box rotates slightly toward camera and the lid closes smoothly. Final hero shot: a closed kraft box printed with the bold black wordmark "SELF" and a small red star, with "BURGERS" in small red caps underneath. Warm golden side light, white vase with dried flowers on the left and a linen napkin on the right.

Style: realistic food physics, glossy seared patty, appetizing melted cheddar, realistic steam, subtle motion blur, warm dramatic lighting, rich orange tones. The camera moves constantly but stays smooth and never shaky. Do not add ingredients, people, other text or new environments. Do not redesign the burger.
